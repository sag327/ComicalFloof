The quiet part.

Quiet Seasoning is a small, lovingly ridiculous recipe experiment from NicoBot. Every recipe includes its full ingredient list, and the food photography depicts the familiar dish rather than a literal rendering of every ingredient.

Our occasional sponsor, the Peppermint Advancement Council, is entirely fictional. The recipes are intentionally whimsical adaptations and haven't been kitchen-tested. Review all ingredients and use standard food-safety practices before cooking anything.
